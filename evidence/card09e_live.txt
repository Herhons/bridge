## 1) 第 4 条：ChatGPT `--new` 清残留草稿 —— 通过（EXIT=0）

复现手法：先在**新对话页**的输入框里塞一条不发送的草稿（`card09e_seed_draft.py`），
再跑一次 `chatgpt_use --new`。

```
$ py -3.10 -X utf8 evidence/card09e_seed_draft.py "DRAFT-09E-MUST-BE-CLEARED"
{"ok": true, "seeded": "DRAFT-09E-MUST-BE-CLEARED",
 "readback": "请只原样回复这一行：BR9-chatgpt-0DRAFT-09E-MUST-BE-CLEARED9e-20261102T1707-85a0",
 "before": "请只原样回复这一行：BR9-chatgpt-09e-20261102T1707-85a0", "url": "https://chatgpt.com/"}

$ py -3.10 -X utf8 src/scripts/chatgpt_use.py "请只原样回复这一行：BR9-chatgpt-09e-20261102T1708-961a" \
      --new --purpose test --timeout 240 --force          # EXIT=0
{"ok": true, "submission": "accepted", "sent": true, "stage": "answered",
 "reply": "BR9-chatgpt-09e-20261102T1708-961a", "elapsed": 8.6, "mode": "new",
```

`draft_on_new` 只取三个值：`cleared`（清掉过草稿）/ `already_empty`（本来就空）/
